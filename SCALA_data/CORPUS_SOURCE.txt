SCALA playbook corpus: source and provenance
============================================

The playbook corpus is not included in this package. It is the public collection
"awesome-playbooks", assembled for:

  Schlette, D. et al. (2024) 'Do you play it by the books? A study on incident
  response playbooks and influencing factors', 2024 IEEE Symposium on Security and
  Privacy (SP). IEEE, pp. 3625-3643. doi:10.1109/SP54263.2024.00060

Repository:      https://github.com/luduslibrum/awesome-playbooks
Clone command:   git clone https://github.com/luduslibrum/awesome-playbooks.git

Revision used by SCALA
----------------------
Commit hash: bc0f6065d79f5330ad6c6a7e4cfcd2e31cce511f (17 July 2023)
Clone date: 26 September 2026 (the same revision was used for all results)

Anyone cloning that commit obtains the identical 2,854 files; a later revision may
contain more or different playbooks and would change the counts below.

What SCALA used from it
-----------------------
Files in the collection at the recorded revision:   2,854
Parsed by SCALA (structure verified against raw):   472
    playbooks/xsoar and playbooks/demisto  (Cortex XSOAR / Demisto YAML)   189
    playbooks/ms_azure                     (Microsoft Sentinel ARM JSON)    193
    playbooks/cisa and other BPMN files    (BPMN 2.0 XML)                    90
The remaining files are documentation, images, or formats that embed their logic in
program code (for example Splunk SOAR, Shuffle, Tines) and were not parsed. The full
per-file disposition is in inventory/SCALA_Playbook_Inventory_472.xlsx.

The ten reference playbooks in the code bundle (real_derived/) are copies of files
from playbooks/xsoar and playbooks/ms_azure; their source paths are listed in
real_derived/MODIFICATION_LOG.md.

Why the corpus is not redistributed
-----------------------------------
The files belong to their vendors and to the collection's maintainers and carry
their own licences. Pointing at the repository and the commit hash gives anyone
the identical corpus without SCALA passing on other people's content.
